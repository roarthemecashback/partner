Cashback Eligibility

  • Christmas event: only theme purchases made from November 15 to December 31, 2026 are eligible for the 25% cashback.
  • The cashback is 25% of the listed price on the Shopify Theme Store (US location) at the time of purchase. For instance, if you buy our Concept theme for $400, you'll receive $100 as cashback.
  • The cashback is only applicable for theme purchases made no more than 30 days prior to submitting the form. For example, if you purchased the theme on November 20 and submit the request after December 20, it will not be valid.
  • Cashback processing typically occurs within the first 10 days of the month following your submission. For instance, if you submit the form in December, the cashback will be processed within the first 10 days of January.
  • We only provide cashback through PayPal. Any PayPal transaction fees are the responsibility of the recipient (you), not us.
  • We reserve the right to withdraw your cashback eligibility if any fraudulent or suspicious activity is detected in your purchases. In the past, a client made multiple purchases using a stolen credit card and Shopify blocked their account afterwards. As such, the cashback is refundable under such circumstances.
